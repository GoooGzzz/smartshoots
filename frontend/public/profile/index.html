BEFORE WE START

A few quick answers.

How do I get a quote?

Share the format, expected shooting duration and number of videos. The quote depends on your project's scope.

Does an enquiry reserve a date?

No. A date is confirmed after availability is checked and the project details are agreed.

Can I shoot more than one video in a session?

We'll discuss the number and length of your videos while planning, so we can allow enough time.
